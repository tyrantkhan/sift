# Privacy

sift collects no data. It makes no network requests and has no telemetry or analytics.

Your reviews stay on your machine, under `~/.local/share/sift` (or `SIFT_HOME`); deleting that folder removes them. Your decisions go back to the coding agent you use, whose provider handles them under its own terms.

Questions go to [the issue tracker](https://github.com/tyrantkhan/sift/issues).
